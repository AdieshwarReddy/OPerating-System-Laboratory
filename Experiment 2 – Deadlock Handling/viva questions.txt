Question	Answer
What is deadlock?	A condition where processes wait indefinitely for resources held by one another.
What are the four deadlock conditions?	Mutual exclusion, hold and wait, no preemption and circular wait.
What is deadlock detection?	Identifying whether a deadlock exists in the system.
What is deadlock prevention?	Eliminating at least one necessary deadlock condition.
What is deadlock recovery?	Resolving a deadlock through process termination or resource preemption.
What is a victim process?	A process selected for termination or resource preemption during recovery.
What is circular wait?	A cycle of processes waiting for resources held by one another.
